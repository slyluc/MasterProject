## Mechanics behind rise and fall of diversity NO CYCLES ALLOWED
At the core of the model is what enviornment allows for high diversity. To analyse this we try to find why and how high diversity can appear, and also how and why it collapses.

### Generator of high diversity.

- Inspecting high diversity, it is apperent that high patchiness appears first, which seems to lay the ground for diversity to also rise.
- Inspecting what creates patchiness seems to multi species activity. It was for p = 0.3, gamma = 0.03, alpha = 0.0125 inspected that even without cycles a link of A-> B-> C allowes wave like dynamics in which pathces are created.
  - Specifically C seems to start of as a larger cluster, which B beginds eating away at. A follows right behind B eating it up, but since A can't eat C, small pokets of C gets left behind inside A even when all of B has been eaten up. This effect seems somewhat enhanced with higher p, but the reason is still un answeared.

### Generator of diversity collaps

- Inspecting diversity collaps, it is apparent that this effect is mainly driven by one dominating species appearing, the ideal example is a new species appearing that can eat the former largest species and some of the other species, without being more prone to eating than the prior largest species.
  
### Following questions.
- These dynamics seem quite random, and can happen one after eachother, so why do we see sharp periods of diversity emergence and sharp preiods of diversity collaps, and not just randomly shifting diversity and pathiness?
  - Some underlying feature must push systems more towards rise than collaps and vise versa.
